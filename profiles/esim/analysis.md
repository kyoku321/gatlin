# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of eSIM, iSIM, and remote SIM provisioning developments for readers who work on mobile connectivity, MVNO/eSIM/IoT services, and carrier businesses, and who track KDDI, SoftBank, NTT DOCOMO, Rakuten Mobile, Transatel, and overseas operators.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Major moves that reshape the eSIM landscape: new GSMA specifications or breaking standards changes, large-scale eSIM platform or MVNE/MVNO launches, regulatory mandates for eSIM support, strategic deals between operators and eSIM providers that change market structure, or security flaws affecting eUICC/remote provisioning at scale.
- **7-8: High value.** Notable eSIM/iSIM product or platform launches, new operator eSIM support or transfer flows, travel and roaming eSIM services with concrete pricing, eSIM IoT/M2M deployments, eUICC/iSIM chip announcements, and market data or certification news worth prompt attention.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental eSIM feature updates, regional availability expansions, moderate plan or pricing changes, and vendor tooling releases with limited competitive impact.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine activation tips, consumer how-to guides, minor device compatibility notes, and self-promotional vendor content.
- **0-2: Noise.** Off-topic material, spam, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Prioritize items with direct competitive relevance to KDDI, SoftBank, NTT DOCOMO, Rakuten Mobile, Transatel and the eSIM/MVNO/IoT connectivity business. Consider technical specificity, standards impact, novelty, source quality, and concrete details such as operators, markets, standards versions, and numbers. Consumer eSIM how-tos and gadget coverage are low priority unless they affect operator strategy or provisioning business. Do not reward exaggerated headlines.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
