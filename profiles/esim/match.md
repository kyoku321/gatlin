# eSIM & iSIM profile

Use this profile when the item's primary subject is eSIM, iSIM, or remote SIM provisioning: eSIM profiles and activation, transfer or migration between devices, GSMA remote SIM provisioning standards (SGP.22, SGP.32, SM-DP+, SM-SR, eUICC), consumer and travel eSIM services and pricing, eSIM-based MVNO or connectivity platforms, eSIM/iSIM management for IoT and M2M, eUICC and iSIM chip technology, and eSIM-related regulation, certification, or operator support.

Choose this profile over a general telecom-news profile when eSIM/iSIM is the central topic of the item. Do not use it for carrier news that merely mentions eSIM in passing, nor for satellite/NTN connectivity items (the `ntn` profile covers those).
