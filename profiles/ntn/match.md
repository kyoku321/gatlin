# NTN & satellite connectivity profile

Use this profile when the item's primary subject is non-terrestrial networks (NTN) and satellite connectivity: direct-to-device (D2D/DTC) and satellite-to-mobile services, LEO/MEO/GEO constellations and their operators (Starlink, AST SpaceMobile, Amazon Kuiper, OneWeb, Telesat), satellite-operator and telco partnerships, 3GPP NTN standards (Release 17/18/19) and NTN-capable chipsets, satellite IoT and backhaul, and satellite spectrum, licensing, or regulatory developments.

Choose this profile over a general telecom-news profile when satellite/NTN connectivity is the central topic of the item. Do not use it for carrier news that merely mentions satellite coverage in passing, nor for eSIM/iSIM provisioning items (the `esim` profile covers those).
