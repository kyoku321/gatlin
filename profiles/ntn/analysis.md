# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of non-terrestrial network (NTN) and satellite connectivity developments for readers who work on mobile connectivity, MVNO/IoT services, and carrier businesses, and who track KDDI, SoftBank, NTT DOCOMO, Rakuten Mobile, Transatel, and overseas operators.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Major moves that reshape satellite-to-mobile connectivity: commercial direct-to-device service launches or nationwide shutdowns, large constellation or spectrum acquisitions, regulators opening satellite spectrum or mandating D2D support, and strategic operator-constellation partnerships that change market structure.
- **7-8: High value.** Notable D2D/DTC trials or commercial milestones, new NTN-capable chipsets or devices, 3GPP NTN standards progress, satellite IoT/backhaul deployments, constellation launch or capacity news, and market or pricing data worth prompt attention.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental constellation progress, regional coverage expansions, moderate satellite service or plan updates, and vendor releases with limited competitive impact.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine launch coverage, generic space-industry news without connectivity relevance, and self-promotional vendor content.
- **0-2: Noise.** Off-topic material, pure astronomy or launch-spectacle coverage, spam, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Prioritize items with direct competitive relevance to KDDI, SoftBank, NTT DOCOMO, Rakuten Mobile, Transatel and the satellite/NTN connectivity business. Consider technical specificity (bands, standards releases, latency, data rates), operator involvement, novelty, source quality, and concrete details such as partners, markets, and numbers. General space or launch news is low priority unless it affects connectivity service or operator strategy. Do not reward exaggerated headlines.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
