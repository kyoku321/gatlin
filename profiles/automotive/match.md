# Automotive industry profile

Use this profile when the item's primary value is understanding the automotive industry as a business and technology sector rather than an individual vehicle.

Typical items include OEM strategy and restructuring, electrification and EV roadmaps, software-defined vehicles and in-car software, ADAS and autonomous driving, battery and powertrain technology, supply chains and semiconductors, plant and capacity decisions, earnings, guidance, sales and production data, joint ventures, partnerships, and M&A, and industry regulation such as emissions rules, EV incentives and mandates, tariffs, and safety standards.

Route product and vehicle news elsewhere. Do not use this profile for new model launches, debuts, concept-car reveals, auto-show and exhibition coverage, test drives, vehicle reviews, pricing or dealer promotions, or motorsports. Those items are not industry-intelligence signals and should be scored low by this profile's analysis rubric.
