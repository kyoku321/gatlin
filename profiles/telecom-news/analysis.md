# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely telecommunications industry news for readers who work on or compete in mobile connectivity, MVNO/eSIM/IoT services, and carrier businesses, and who track KDDI, SoftBank, NTT DOCOMO, Rakuten Mobile, Transatel, and overseas operators.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Major industry moves that reshape the carrier or MVNO landscape: large mergers and acquisitions, spectrum auctions and regulatory decisions, nationwide network launches or shutdowns, strategic partnerships between major operators, disruptive pricing moves, or technology shifts affecting MVNO/IoT connectivity business models.
- **7-8: High value.** Notable operator or vendor announcements, new MVNO/MVNE market entries or deals, 5G/6G and Open RAN deployments, eSIM/iSIM launches, IoT connectivity platform news, analyst-grade market data, and regulatory developments worth prompt attention.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental network progress, local-market operator news, moderate product or plan updates, and vendor releases with limited competitive impact.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine service announcements, minor handset launches, self-promotional vendor content, consumer plan marketing.
- **0-2: Noise.** Off-topic material, consumer gadget reviews, spam, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Prioritize items with direct competitive relevance to KDDI, SoftBank, NTT DOCOMO, Rakuten Mobile, Transatel and the MVNO/IoT connectivity business. Consider strategic impact on carriers and MVNOs, novelty, source quality, and concrete details such as operators, markets, technologies, and numbers. Handset and gadget news is low priority unless it affects operator strategy or network business. Do not reward exaggerated headlines.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
