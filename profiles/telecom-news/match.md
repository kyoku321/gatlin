# Telecom industry news profile

Use this profile as the default for timely developments in the telecommunications industry: mobile network operators (MNOs), MVNOs and MVNEs, 5G and 6G, radio access and core networks, IoT connectivity, enterprise connectivity, mobile services and pricing, spectrum and regulation, network vendors, and the carrier ecosystem, with particular attention to KDDI, SoftBank, NTT DOCOMO, Rakuten Mobile, Transatel, and overseas carriers.

Do not use this profile when the item's primary subject is eSIM/iSIM or remote SIM provisioning (use the `esim` profile) or satellite/NTN connectivity such as direct-to-device and constellations (use the `ntn` profile). Use this profile for other carrier and telecom news that only mentions those topics in passing.
